When you unlock Lai'tela, your Antimatter Dimensions and Tickspeed Upgrades switch to a new mode of production called **Continuum**. Continuum gives the same effect as before but allows for fractional purchases — partial Dimension or Tickspeed Upgrades. These fractional purchases are given for free without spending antimatter, and provide a proportional share of their multiplier.

The purchase buttons for Antimatter Dimensions and Tickspeed Upgrades change to display the number of upgrades you would buy if Continuum were off, and the purchase count scales smoothly with antimatter. Example: with **2e7** antimatter, you'll get a Continuum value of about **5.3** for tickspeed (initial cost **1000**, ×**10** per upgrade) since you can buy it **5** times and are about **30%** of the way to the next. Tickspeed Continuum then gives a boost equal to **(upgrade multiplier)^5.3**.

Some upgrades multiply Continuum value directly, which boosts production without affecting cost scaling. These upgrades **will not function if Continuum is disabled** on the Autobuyers page — disabling Continuum can result in a loss of production. Continuum makes the Antimatter Dimension and Tickspeed autobuyers obsolete, so all their related autobuyer settings are hidden on the Autobuyers tab while Continuum is active.
