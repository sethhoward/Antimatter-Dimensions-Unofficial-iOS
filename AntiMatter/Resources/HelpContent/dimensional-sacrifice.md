**Dimensional Sacrifice unlocks after your fifth Dimension Boost.**

Sacrificing immediately resets the owned quantity of all non-8th Dimensions to zero, without reducing the multiplier or current cost. In return, it multiplies the 8th Dimension Multiplier by the displayed value. It takes time to get back to your previous production, but you end up with a net increase.

The Sacrifice multiplier scales with the number of 1st Dimensions you had at the time of sacrifice, and the scaling improves with certain Achievements and challenge rewards. The multiplier is kept across sacrifices — sacrificing once at **×10** and then once at **×4** gives the same total as **×8** then **×5**: in both cases you end up at **×40**.
