Autobuyers automatically purchase Dimensions, upgrades, or trigger prestiges. All of them live in the **Autobuyers** subtab of the **Automation** tab.

Antimatter Dimension autobuyers and the Tickspeed autobuyer can be unlocked just by reaching total antimatter thresholds. Most other autobuyers require completing challenges or buying specific upgrades.

## Common controls

- **Interval** — cooldown before the autobuyer tries another purchase. Dimension + Tickspeed autobuyer intervals can only be upgraded once their associated challenge has been beaten.
- **Bulk Buy** — once an Antimatter Dimension autobuyer's interval reaches its minimum (**100 ms**), further upgrades instead double the max amount purchasable per tick. Can be disabled.
- **Buy quantity (Antimatter Dimensions)** — autobuyers can be set to buy single Dimensions or buy until 10. Bulk buy is disabled when set to singles.
- **Buy quantity (Tickspeed)** — single upgrade per activation, or buy max once Challenge 9 has been beaten.
- **Dimension Boost settings** — set a max boost count, a minimum number of Antimatter Galaxies before boosts are bought regardless, and (when unlocked) an exact-bulk-buy count. Hitting the Galaxy threshold makes the autobuyer ignore the max-boost limit.
- **Max Galaxies** — cap on the Antimatter Galaxy autobuyer.
- **IP on crunch** — once you Break Infinity, you can require a minimum IP gain before the autobuyer crunches.
- **Sacrifice** — this autobuyer's interval is already maxed, so it can fire every tick.
- **Dynamic Amount** — upgraded prestige autobuyers have a threshold-trigger mode. Turning on Dynamic Amount makes the threshold auto-increase when you unlock upgrades or achievements that multiply it.

## Master switches

- **Pause/Resume** — pauses or resumes all autobuyers without changing individual settings.
- **Enable/Disable All** — toggles every autobuyer's individual on/off switch.
