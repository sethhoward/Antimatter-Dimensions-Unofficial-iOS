Once you're able to gain at least **1e160 Reality Machines** in a single Reality, you unlock a new resource — **Imaginary Machines** (iM). RM also becomes hard-capped at **1e160**: you can no longer gain any more past that limit.

Unlocking iM opens the **Imaginary Upgrades** tab. Each upgrade has a condition you must fulfill to unlock plus an iM cost to actually purchase. The first two rows of upgrades can be repeatedly bought. The other three rows are one-time upgrades.

## Two iM values

- **iM Cap** — the maximum amount of iM you can ever have, based on the highest RM amount you *could* have gotten if there were no RM cap. This updates continuously, so it immediately increases if you ever surpass your previous highest uncapped RM.
- **Current iM** — passively rises toward your iM cap, slowing down exponentially as it approaches the cap. By default, the *gap* between your current iM and your cap halves every minute. This growth rate is unaffected by any modifiers to game speed.

Imaginary Upgrades will eventually unlock the final two Celestials.
