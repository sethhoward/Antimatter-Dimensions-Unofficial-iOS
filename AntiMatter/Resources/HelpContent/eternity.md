Once you reach **1.8e308 IP**, you can **Eternity**. Eternity resets everything before this point *except* challenge times, Achievements, and the General section of Statistics. You get access to a lot of new content after your first Eternity.

You can pass **1.8e308 IP** without anything being forced on you — unlike the first time you reached **1.8e308** antimatter. The more IP you had before going Eternal, the more Eternity Points you receive. You also gain one "Eternity" (the counter).

EP gain scales similarly to IP gain, but off IP rather than antimatter. The base is roughly **1.62 EP** at **1.8e308 IP**, multiplied by **5** for every factor of **1.8e308** more. The result is always rounded down, so you'll get **1 EP** at the threshold but won't reach **2 EP** until about **1e349 IP**.
