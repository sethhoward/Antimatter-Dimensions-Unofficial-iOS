Purchasing an **Antimatter Galaxy** resets your game to only **4** Dimensions available, but increases the effect of your Tickspeed Upgrades by **+0.02** for your first two Galaxies. As you buy more Galaxies, the multiplier strengthens further.

The first few Tickspeed purchases will see little impact, but the increase is multiplicative and won't take long to show up.

Your first Antimatter Galaxy requires **80** 8th Dimensions, and each additional Galaxy costs **60** more.

## Distant Galaxy scaling

Above **100** Antimatter Galaxies, the cost increase between Galaxies grows by **2** per Galaxy — the next Galaxy costs **62** more, then **64**, and so on.

## Remote Galaxy scaling

Above **800** Antimatter Galaxies, the *total* cost increases by an additional **0.2%** per Galaxy, on top of Distant scaling.
