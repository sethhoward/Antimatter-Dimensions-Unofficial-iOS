**Effarig** is the second Celestial you encounter. They unlock by pouring at least **1e24 RM** into Teresa's container.

Effarig introduces a currency called **Relic Shards**, gained by using different *kinds* of Glyph effects during a Reality. The number of distinct effects active during the Reality strongly affects Relic Shard gain; EP also affects it to a much lesser degree. Relic Shards are the currency for Effarig's unlocks, and you'll gain them from every Reality from now on.

Using Relic Shards, you can purchase multiple upgrades (see **Advanced Glyph Mechanics**) that improve your Glyphs and let you filter them by effect and rarity for fully-automated Realities.

## Effarig's Reality

Effarig's final unlock is their own Reality at **1e20 Relic Shards**. Their Reality is divided into three layers — **Infinity**, **Eternity**, and **Reality**. You must complete each layer before getting access to the next. Completing Effarig's **Eternity** unlocks the next Celestial.

Completing Effarig's Reality unlocks a new Glyph type — **Effarig Glyphs**. They have **7** possible effects, viewable in the Glyph filter settings. You can only have one Effarig Glyph equipped at a time. Without further unlocks, Effarig Glyphs can have at most **4** effects, and the **RM multiplier** and **Glyph instability** effects cannot appear on the same Glyph. Reaching Effarig level **10** within Ra removes both of these restrictions.
