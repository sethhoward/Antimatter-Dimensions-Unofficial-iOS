**Pelle Strikes** are triggered by certain events in the Doomed Reality. The first Strike is encountered by reaching Infinity for the first time within a Doomed Reality. More Strikes occur as you progress further. Each Strike adds a nerf to a specific part of the game — tap the Strike name to see exactly what it does.

Each Pelle Strike also unlocks a **Rift bar**.

Rifts can be activated by tapping them to toggle between **Idle** and **Filling**. Only **two** Rifts can be Filling at any given time. When active, Rifts consume **3%** of a Rift-specific resource per second. Each Rift offers a unique effect based on the total amount filled.

Once you've passed the Eternity Strike, Decay / Collapse / Disarray (whichever it cycles as) caps its effect at **1e2000** drained Replicanti.

Each Rift also offers **three milestone rewards** for filling them up to a certain percentage.
