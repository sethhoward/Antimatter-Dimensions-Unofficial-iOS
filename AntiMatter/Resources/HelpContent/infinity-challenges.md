Infinity Challenges work like Normal Challenges but have higher end goals and are generally harder. Their rewards aren't autobuyers — they're more unique boosts to your various forms of production. As with Normal Challenges, the rightmost column of Infinity Upgrades is disabled inside an Infinity Challenge.

Unlike Normal Challenges (all unlocked at once), each Infinity Challenge requires you to reach a specific antimatter threshold before you can attempt it. The thresholds are listed under each challenge's tile in the Infinity Challenges subtab.
