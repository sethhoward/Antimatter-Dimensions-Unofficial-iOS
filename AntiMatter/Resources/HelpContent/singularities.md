**Singularities** are a resource you obtain using features within Lai'tela.

To get a Singularity, you need to reach **200 Dark Energy**. When you do, you can **condense** all your Dark Energy into a Singularity, resetting Dark Energy back to zero. Any extra Dark Energy above the cap is wasted. Note that only Dark Energy is reset — your Dark Matter and Dark Matter Dimensions are unchanged when condensing.

Once you reach **10** Singularities, you can freely increase or decrease the Dark Energy requirement to condense by a factor of **10** (with a minimum of **200**). This change scales the Singularities-per-condense by *more than* a factor of **10**, making higher caps worth more if you're willing to wait.

## Milestones

Singularities are used to unlock **Singularity Milestones** — similar to Eternity Milestones. You don't spend Singularities to unlock milestones; you just need the total count.

Milestones come in three types: one-time milestones, milestones repeatable a limited number of times, and milestones repeatable indefinitely.

Independently of the type, each milestone has an icon hinting at what kind of upgrade it gives:

- **ᛝ** — helps mechanics specific to Lai'tela.
- A "spread-outward" arrows icon — lets a Lai'tela resource affect the rest of the game.
- A "pull-inward" arrows icon — improves Lai'tela based on something *outside* of Lai'tela.
