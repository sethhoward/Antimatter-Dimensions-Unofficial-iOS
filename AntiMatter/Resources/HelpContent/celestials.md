Once you get all of the Reality Upgrades, the first **Celestial** is unlocked. This adds a new tab next to Reality. The first subtab — **Celestial Navigation** — shows a map that updates as you progress. Only part of the map is visible when first unlocked; new content reveals gradually, typically with a visual indicator of your progress toward the next step.

Each Celestial has unique mechanics and upgrades. You need to defeat all seven to beat the game. Unlocking or defeating a Celestial has different conditions depending on the Celestial.

All Celestials have their own Celestial Reality, but how the Reality matters to each Celestial — and to the rest of the game — depends on the Celestial.

Celestials are timeless entities. Unless otherwise stated, any new mechanics introduced by Celestials are not affected by game speed multipliers and instead refer specifically to **real time** rather than game time.
