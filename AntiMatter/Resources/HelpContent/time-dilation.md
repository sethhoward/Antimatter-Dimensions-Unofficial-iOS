**Time Dilation** is unlocked by purchasing the Time Study just below EC11 and EC12. The study requires **5000** unspent Time Theorems with a tree that can reach it, **13,000 total TT**, and both EC11 and EC12 each completed five times.

Dilating time starts a modified Eternity in which all of your Antimatter / Infinity / Time Dimension multiplier *exponents* and the Tickspeed multiplier *exponent* are raised to **^0.75** — significantly reducing them. If you can still reach **1.8e308 IP** in this Dilated Eternity, you complete it and earn a new resource: **Tachyon Particles**.

You can Dilate as many times as you want, but Tachyon Particles can't be "farmed" like other resources. Tachyon Particles are never reduced — only increased — and the increase is capped by your TP multipliers and the antimatter you reach in the current Dilation. You generally can't increase your TP unless you've gained a TP multiplier *or* you can significantly outperform your previous antimatter inside Dilation.

Tachyon Particles generate another resource called **Dilated Time** (DT). DT converts into **Tachyon Galaxies** by crossing thresholds — similar to free Tickspeed Upgrades from Time Dimensions. Tachyon Galaxies behave like Replicanti Galaxies: they affect tickspeed like Antimatter Galaxies *but do not* increase the cost of your next Antimatter Galaxy.

Unlocking Time Dilation also unlocks upgrades you can purchase with DT. The first and third upgrades in the first row are infinitely rebuyable. The second is also rebuyable but reaches a cap.
