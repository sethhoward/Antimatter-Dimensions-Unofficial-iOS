Antimatter is the resource that drives the entire game. You start with **10** antimatter when you first open the game, and you spend it to buy the 1st Antimatter Dimension.

Antimatter Dimensions are production units. The 1st Antimatter Dimension produces your antimatter. Each higher Antimatter Dimension produces the one below it, giving you steady growth. There are eight Antimatter Dimensions in total.

## Dimension Multiplier

Next to each Dimension's label there's a multiplier (e.g. **1st Dimension ×1.0**). The base production of that Dimension is multiplied by this number. The multiplier increases by **×2** for every **10** of that Dimension you've purchased. Each time it does, the price of that Dimension increases.

## Accumulated quantity

The number you own — a combination of how many you've purchased with antimatter, plus production from the next higher Dimension.

## Purchased quantity

The number in brackets shows how many of that Dimension you've bought toward the next multiplier upgrade. **(4)** means you need **6** more to trigger the next ×2.

## Growth rate

A per-second growth percentage. **100%** means the Dimension is doubling each second — useful for judging overall progress.

## Buy buttons

When the cost button is highlighted, you can buy a single Dimension. When "until 10" is highlighted, you can buy whatever quantity gets you to the next multiplier upgrade for that Dimension. Use the **Buy 10 Until 10** toggle in the dimensions tab to flip between modes.

## Max all

**Max all** buys until 10 of the 1st Antimatter Dimension as long as it can, then the 2nd, and so on up through the 8th. After dimensions, it buys max Tickspeed Upgrades.
