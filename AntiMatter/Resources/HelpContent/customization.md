A short tour of what you can configure. Each item below lives in **Options**, with its own description right next to the control.

- **News Ticker** — toggle the scrolling messages and adjust how fast they move. Some messages unlock achievements as you see them.
- **Notation** — pick how very large numbers are displayed (Scientific, Engineering, Letters, and others).
- **Refresh Rate** — how often the UI updates. Defaults to 30 fps. Lower values save battery.
- **Update Rate** — how often the game simulation ticks. Defaults to 33 ms (≈30 ticks per second). Lower is smoother; higher saves battery.
- **Automatic tab switching** — when on, entering or exiting a challenge automatically jumps to the Antimatter Dimensions tab. Turn off if you'd rather stay where you are.
- **Offline Progress** — toggle whether time spent away from the app advances the simulation when you return. The companion **Offline Ticks** slider controls how many simulation steps the catch-up uses. Higher = more accurate, slower.
- **Automator Log Max** — caps how many Automator log entries stay in memory. Visible only after you unlock the Automator.
- **Hardware Keyboard Shortcuts** — list of every keyboard shortcut the game responds to when an external keyboard is connected. The list filters by what your progression has unlocked.
- **iCloud Sync** — opt-in cross-device sync. See the **Your savefile** entry for the full reconciliation story.
- **Button haptics** — gentle vibration on button taps (iPhone only).
- **Color glyph effect text** — tints active glyph effects in their type's color. Cosmetic.
- **Show altered glyph details** — shows the addition/empower/boost sacrifice thresholds on the Glyphs tab. Only meaningful after unlocking Altered Glyphs (Ra reward).

Everything in this list lives in **Options**.
