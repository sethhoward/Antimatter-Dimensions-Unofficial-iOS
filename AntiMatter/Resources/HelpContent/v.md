**V** is a special Celestial — not unlocked by another Celestial. V is unlocked by completing **Achievement 151** ("You really didn't need it anyway"), which requires reaching **800 Antimatter Galaxies** without buying 8th Antimatter Dimensions in your current Infinity.

After the subtab unlocks from that achievement, V has a second set of requirements before they're fully unlocked. You must have completed **10** Realities and have **10,000 unspent RM**, plus reach **6500 Eternities**, **1e120 Infinities**, **1e8 Dilated Time**, and **1e150 Replicanti** — all in the same Reality.

When all requirements are met, you can enter **V's Reality**.

## V-Achievements

V's Reality is only the beginning. V has six different requirements, each requiring you to make a certain amount of progress within V's Reality. Completing a requirement awards a **V-Achievement**. V-Achievements are permanent — they persist after exiting V's Reality, and they don't all need to be done simultaneously.

After completing a requirement, the goal increases and the V-Achievement can be completed again at the new threshold. You can complete each category of V-Achievement up to **six** times.

Completed V-Achievements do two things:

- At certain totals of V-Achievements, you automatically unlock upgrades on the V tab without spending resources.
- Each V-Achievement gives **one Space Theorem**.

## Goal reduction

The goal-reduction upgrade — unlocked at **2 V-Achievements** — lets you make some V-Achievement requirements easier by spending Perk Points, down to whatever the easiest tier requires. The cost of reducing a goal does not increase as it's used, and reductions apply to future tiers as well.

## Space Theorems

Space Theorems let you buy normally forbidden Time Studies — like multiple paths in the Pace Split after the improved IP formula, or both Time Studies in a dark/light pair. Like Time Theorems, Space Theorems are returned in full when you respec your studies. With enough of them, you can eventually purchase every Time Study at once.

Reaching **36 V-Achievements** (all six categories maxed) unlocks the next Celestial.
