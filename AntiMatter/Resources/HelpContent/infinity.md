Once your antimatter passes 2^1024 (around **1.8e308**) — sometimes called "Infinity" — the world can no longer contain it, and you're forced into a **Big Crunch**. This resets your antimatter, Antimatter Dimensions, Dimension Boosts, and Antimatter Galaxies. The act of crunching is also called "Infinitying".

You'll eventually be able to pass **1.8e308**, but until then any larger value displays as **Infinity**.

Each Infinity gives one **Infinity Point** (IP), spendable on upgrades in the new Infinity tab — purchased top to bottom. You also gain one "Infinity", which is effectively your crunch counter.

The "Multiply Infinity Points from all sources by 2" upgrade can be bought multiple times, but each purchase costs **10×** more IP than the last. You must complete the Achievement "No DLC required" before you can start buying this upgrade.
