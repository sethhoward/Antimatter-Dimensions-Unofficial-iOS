Alchemy Resources can be combined together in certain reactions to create new **compound resources**, unlocked at higher Effarig levels. Reactions run once per Reality and are unaffected by real-time amplification.

Reactions have higher yields and run faster when your reagent amounts are higher. The cap on a compound resource is equal to the lowest cap among its reagents. For a reaction to occur, the current amount of every reagent must be greater than the current amount of the produced compound.

## How speed scales

Reaction speed is proportional to the amount of *usable* reagents — but only the reagent amounts *above* the current product amount count.

- With **10,000** of all reagents and **7500** of the product, only **2500** of the reagents are eligible — that's what determines the reaction speed.
- With **0** of the product, *all* reagent is eligible and the reaction runs **×4** faster.
- With **10,000** of the product, no reagent can be used and the reaction does not run at all.

## Activating reactions

Tap the circle for a reaction's product to toggle activation. When a reaction is running, you'll see moving lines from the reagents to the product. If a connection is drawn as a *solid* line, that reagent's current amount is too low (relative to the product's amount) for the reaction to proceed.
