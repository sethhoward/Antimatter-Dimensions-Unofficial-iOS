To make Eternities faster, you unlock buffs as you accumulate more "Eternity". Most milestones either let you start with upgrades you'd otherwise lose, give you new autobuyers for further automation, or unlock passive offline resource gain at a reduced rate.

Milestones that grant upgrades will auto-purchase and max those upgrades at the start of each new Eternity, effectively making them permanent.

New autobuyers from milestones have toggles next to their manual buttons (e.g. Infinity Dimension autobuyers appear on the Infinity Dimensions tab) in addition to their entries on the Autobuyers tab. Milestones that improve the Dimension Boost, Antimatter Galaxy, and Big Crunch autobuyers update those existing entries on the Autobuyers tab.

The passive-generation milestones only work *offline* by design and may need specific autobuyer settings to function properly. The milestone description on the Eternity Milestones tab lists the required setup.
