**Perks** unlock with Reality. Each Perk has a unique effect, but most are quality-of-life improvements. Every Perk costs just **1 Perk Point** to buy.

You gain **1 Perk Point** per Reality, spent on the Perks tree. You start at "You can now choose from 4 Glyphs on Reality" and can only unlock Perks directly adjacent to ones you already own. The tree contains loops, which you can traverse in either direction.

Perk nodes come in two shapes:

- **Circle** — a normal Perk.
- **Diamond** — also gives **Automator Points** in addition to its normal effect.

Node colours roughly indicate which part of the game the Perk affects most.
