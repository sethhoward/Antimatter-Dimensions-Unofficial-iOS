Most upgrades and effects in Antimatter Dimensions fall into three categories.

- **Additive**: written with a **+** (or the word "increase") followed by a number. Multiple additive effects sum up. These sometimes also show up as subtractive cost reductions.
- **Multiplicative**: written with a **×** (or the word "multiply") followed by a number, or sometimes as two numbers joined by **➜**. Different multiplicative sources always combine by multiplying. Negative effects in this category apply as division.
- **Power**: written with **^** followed by a number. Power effects apply in sequence — equivalent to multiplying their values together and applying a single final power. Negative effects here are powers less than 1.

Unless an upgrade or reward explicitly says it *replaces* an older value, every effect stacks. When an upgrade does replace a value, the replacement happens *before* the categories are applied. To get the final result, combine each category individually, then apply them in this order: additive, then multiplicative, then power.

Dilation (and any Dilation-like effect) applies *after* all of the above are stacked.

Glyph effects have two stacking layers — how they stack with each other, and how the total stacks with the rest of the game. These don't have to match. For example, the **Antimatter Dimension Power** glyph effect stacks *additively with itself*, but the total is then added to a base of **1** and applied as a *power effect* to Antimatter Dimensions.
