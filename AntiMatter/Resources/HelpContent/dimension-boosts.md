A **Dimension Boost** resets your antimatter and Antimatter Dimensions, but unlocks another Antimatter Dimension and boosts your Dimension multipliers.

The 1st Dimension Boost requires **20** 4th Dimensions; the 2nd requires **20** 5th Dimensions; and so on. After unlocking all **8** Dimensions, each further boost costs **15** more 8th Dimensions than the last and no longer unlocks a Dimension — but continues to add to your Dimension multipliers.

You gain a **×2** multiplier to the 1st Dimension for every Dimension Boost you have. Each higher Dimension gets the multiplier applied one fewer time, down to **0**. With 3 Boosts: 1st Dimension **×8**, 2nd **×4**, 3rd **×2**, and the rest unaffected.
