Replicanti is another resource you unlock at **1e140 IP**. Rather than producing something else, Replicanti produces *itself* up to a maximum of **1.8e308**. They produce at their own pace, unaffected by Tickspeed Upgrades. Each individual Replicanti has an initial **1%** chance of producing another every Replicanti tick (initially every second), and both the chance and the interval can be upgraded with IP.

If you've purchased the Replicanti Galaxy upgrade, you can spend a full Replicanti count to gain a "free" Replicanti Galaxy — your Replicanti count resets to **1**. This Galaxy acts as if it were an Antimatter Galaxy *but does not increase the cost of the next Antimatter Galaxy*. It still resets the same things an Antimatter Galaxy resets.

Replicanti give a multiplier to all Infinity Dimensions, reaching a maximum of about **×1.05 million** at **1.8e308** Replicanti.

## Upgrade costs

- **Chance** — Base **1e150 IP**, ×**1e15 IP** per upgrade.
- **Interval** — Base **1e140 IP**, ×**1e10 IP** per upgrade.
- **Galaxy** — Base **1e170 IP**, ×**1e25 IP** per upgrade plus an additional **×100,000 IP** per upgrade (scaling like Distant Antimatter Galaxies). Above **100** Replicanti Galaxies the per-upgrade additive grows to **×1e55**. Above **1000** the scaling switches from quadratic to cubic, with the **×1e55** multiplier itself increasing by **×1e5** per upgrade.
