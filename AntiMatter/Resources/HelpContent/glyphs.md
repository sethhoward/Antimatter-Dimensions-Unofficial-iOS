A **Glyph** is an equippable item with four attributes.

- **Type** — the Glyph's flavour (e.g. "Glyph of Power"). This determines which effects it can have.
- **Level** — how strong the Glyph is. Scales with how much of various resources you obtained in the Reality you got it from.
- **Rarity** — a percentage between **0%** and **100%** that also affects strength. Random per Glyph, but influenced by various upgrades. Ranges are named (Common, Uncommon, etc.).
- **Effects** — the boosts you get from equipping it. A Glyph can have up to four effects. Higher level and rarity tend to mean more effects.

Your first Glyph has a fixed effect and rarity, but its level scales with your progression before any Reality content. Once you receive a Glyph, its attributes can't be changed.

## Equipping

Tap a Glyph in your inventory to open its details, then **Equip**. You can also drag a Glyph from the inventory to an empty active slot. When equipped, Glyph icons become circular and their effects are added to the **Current Effects** panel.

Equipping multiple Glyphs with the same effect combines them: effects with **+** sum their values; effects with **×** multiply.

You can equip Glyphs into *empty* slots at any time during a Reality. Dragging a Glyph into an already-occupied slot will *swap* the equipped one — but that restarts the current Reality.

## Protected rows

The first rows of your inventory are **protected** slots. New Glyphs are never placed there (even if your inventory is full), and Sort / Auto-clean buttons leave them alone. The number of protected rows is adjustable via the stepper on the Glyphs tab. If your inventory fills up, any new Glyphs are automatically deleted (or sacrificed if you've unlocked sacrifice).

## Deleting and sacrifice

Tap any inventory Glyph to open the details sheet — sacrifice or move-to-protected-row actions are there. Until you've unlocked Glyph Sacrifice from a Reality Upgrade, **deleting Glyphs gives no benefit** beyond freeing inventory space.

## Glyph selection on Reality

Once you've unlocked Glyph Sacrifice, you can disable the Glyph Selection modal that appears at the start of each Reality. With it disabled, completing a Reality will pick a random Glyph from the available options.

## Inspecting

Tap any group of circular Glyphs (equipped, sacrifice records, upcoming peek) to bring up a detailed summary that shows all of them at once with shorter descriptions — handy for sharing with others.
