When you reach **1e4000 EP** and have completed the first **13** rows of Achievements, you'll be able to purchase the Time Study that unlocks **Reality**. Unlocking it opens a new tab where you can find the button to make a new Reality.

Starting a new Reality resets almost the entire game up to this point, but in exchange grants you a new currency — **Reality Machines** (RM) — plus a Glyph and a Perk Point.

Unlike previous resets, you also lose the first **13** rows of Achievements (all pre-Reality Achievements and their rewards). You still keep all values under the **General** section of Statistics and all your best challenge times.

After your first Reality, the Glyphs tab gives you a button to restart the current Reality without changing the upcoming Glyph choices. **This restart does not award any rewards — even if you'd otherwise be able to complete the Reality.**

You need to redo each Achievement's requirements to re-earn the reward. You also passively unlock the next incomplete Achievement every **30 minutes** without any effort, even if you don't meet the requirement. This automatic completion can be disabled, in which case the timer counts down to zero and pauses, immediately completing another Achievement when unpaused. The timer continues to progress at the same rate while offline.

## Resources

- **Reality Machines** are spent on different upgrades throughout the Reality tab and are your primary currency from this point on.
- **Glyphs** are equippable items you must place in slots to use their boosts.
- **Perk Points** are spent in the Perks subtab.

## Scaling

RM scales purely off EP. The Reality button tells you how much EP you need for the next RM. The first **10** RM scale linearly in the exponent between **1e4000 EP** and **1e16000/3 EP**; past that, **RM = 1000^(log₁₀(EP) / 4000 − 1)**, which exceeds linear gain above the threshold.

Glyph level scales off a combination of EP, Replicanti, and Dilated Time, with a minimum of **1**. Glyph type, effects, and rarity are randomised.

You always gain **1 Perk Point** per Reality.
