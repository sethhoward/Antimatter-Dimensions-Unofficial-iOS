A **Time Study** is a powerful post-Eternity upgrade, purchased with a new resource called **Time Theorems**. Time Studies can boost almost anything you've seen so far in the game, and a few of them change the way certain formulas work.

Time Theorems are limited and cost more for each one you buy. They can be purchased with antimatter, IP, or EP — each currency type has its own cost scaling. Time Theorems do *not* reset on Eternity.

## Tree layout

Studies are arranged like a tree. You must buy a study's prerequisites before continuing. Only the very top study is available at first; from there you can buy any study directly below one you already own and can afford. There are three exceptions:

- Where the lines between studies are *colored*, you can only choose one of the three paths at a time.
- When an Eternity Challenge study is in the way, you need to *complete* all challenges connected to it at least once before you can access the study below. You don't need the EC study itself bought first.
- Near the bottom, where the paths join, you can only pick one study out of each pair.

## Long-press to fill

Long-press on any study to attempt to buy all prerequisites up to that point. The path-respecting auto-buy will follow your preferences (see **Preferences** below) for split choices; if you don't have a preference set, it stops at any unresolved split. It will also stop if you can't afford all the required Time Theorems.

## Presets

The six numbered buttons let you save your current set of studies into a slot for quick re-loading later. Tap to load; long-press the menu icon for Save / Load / Edit / Export / Delete. Presets can be renamed — but each name must be unique.

## Import Tree / Edit Preset

The edit/import sheet shows which studies a preset would buy when loaded, alongside any errors. Split paths can be referred to by the path's *name* — instead of writing `71, 81, 91, 101` you can write `antimatter` to mean the full Antimatter split. Appending `!` to a string containing a valid Eternity Challenge will make the import attempt to enter that EC immediately.

## Preferences

The path-preference sheet lets you choose default paths for the three-way splits. The Dimension split allows two paths once you've purchased the relevant Time Study. Preferences are used both by long-press auto-buy and by preset imports.

## Respecs

A respec resets your tree and returns every spent Time Theorem. It only triggers when finishing an Eternity — you cannot respec studies mid-Eternity.

## Theorem costs

- **Antimatter** — Initially **1e20000**, ×**1e20000** per Theorem.
- **Infinity Points** — Initially **1**, ×**1e100** per Theorem.
- **Eternity Points** — Initially **1**, ×**2** per Theorem.
