**Tesseracts** are a resource you unlock by completing The Nameless Ones' Reality.

Infinity Dimensions are normally capped at **2,000** total purchases, which caps how large their multipliers can get because eventually you can't upgrade them any more. Tesseracts raise this cap by spending Infinity Points.

The cost of Tesseracts increases super-exponentially, but each successive Tesseract is significantly stronger than the last to make up for it. Tesseract count is **never reset** — once purchased, you don't need to reach the IP cost again to take advantage of the raised cap in later Realities.

The Infinity Dimensions tab shows your current Tesseract count and the cost of the next one. Your IP display will also show a percentage toward the next Tesseract. When you can afford a Tesseract, the post-break Big Crunch button highlights and brings you to the Infinity Dimension tab when tapped.
