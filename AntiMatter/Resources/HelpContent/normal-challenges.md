Normal Challenges unlock after your first Infinity. They change in-game mechanics in different ways to make Infinity harder. To complete a challenge, you must reach **1.8e308** antimatter again under its modified rules.

Each completed Normal Challenge awards either a new autobuyer or an upgrade to an existing one. You can run them multiple times — but only the first completion grants the reward. You can exit a challenge at any time via the **Exit Challenge** button.

Your first Infinity counts as the first Normal Challenge — so it's already complete when you unlock the challenges tab.

The rightmost column of Infinity Upgrades is disabled while inside any challenge.
