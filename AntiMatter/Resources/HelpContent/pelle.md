**Pelle** is unlocked by purchasing the last Imaginary Upgrade. Pelle's tab contains a button to **Doom your Reality**. To Doom your Reality, you must have completed all **17** rows of Achievements available to you, plus attained **25,000** of each Alchemy Resource.

Dooming starts a new **Doomed Reality**, resetting almost the entire game up to Reality — and giving you no rewards from your current Reality's progress.

When you enter the Doomed Reality, you keep all values under the **General** and **Reality** sections of the Statistics tab, plus all your best Challenge times. Inside Doomed Realities, many upgrades, Time Studies, Challenge rewards, Celestial rewards, Perks, and other mechanics are disabled or grant no reward. View the **Show effects in Doomed Reality** sheet on the Pelle tab for the full list.

## Remnants and Reality Shards

**Remnants** are a new currency gained on **Armageddon** resets. Remnant gain is based on your best ever antimatter, IP, and EP across all Doomed Realities. Remnants produce **Reality Shards**, which can be spent on **Pelle Upgrades**.

## Pelle Upgrades

Pelle Upgrades fall into two categories. The five upgrades in the **first row** are repeatedly buyable but eventually cap out. They boost different aspects of the game, easing progression within Doomed Realities.

The upgrades in the lower rows offer automation and quality-of-life improvements. Things unlocked from these upgrades **cannot be unlocked by their usual methods** — for instance, no Normal Challenges grant autobuyers in a Doomed Reality, because the autobuyers are gated behind Pelle Upgrades instead. You can toggle a button above the upgrades panel to show or hide bought upgrades.
