**The Nameless Ones** are the third Celestial, unlocked by completing Effarig's **Eternity**.

When you unlock them, you immediately gain access to two new time-related mechanics. You can store **game time** by charging your Black Hole, and you can store **real time** by intentionally halting your production. Stored game time is also used as a currency for purchasing unlocks from The Nameless Ones.

## Charging your Black Hole

Charging your Black Hole gives you stored game time at the expense of setting your game speed to **1**. The game effectively uses its increased game speed to store game time. The main use is to *discharge* — spend your stored game time to skip forward in time by that duration. Discharging is *not* subject to any modifiers to game speed at the time of use, only when the time is stored.

## Storing real time

Storing real time completely stops all production, effectively pausing your game. For every real-time second that passes, you gain stored real time (with an efficiency factor).

You can use stored real time to **amplify** a Reality on the Glyphs tab. When you complete the Reality, all your stored real time is used at once to repeat that exact Reality over and over — giving you all the rewards you would have gained from each repetition.

Example: with **50 minutes** stored, amplifying a Reality that lasted **10 minutes** and gave **1e30 RM** + **1e12 Relic Shards** will produce **5e30 RM**, **5e12 Relic Shards**, **5 Glyphs** (subject to your filter settings), and **5 Perk Points**.

If your Reality lasted less than **1 second**, the amplification factor is capped by the seconds stored. Example: with **1000 seconds** stored amplifying a Reality lasting **0.2 seconds**, you'll use **200 seconds** to simulate **1000** Realities.

You can toggle a setting that automatically stores offline time as stored real time.

## Unlocks

- **First unlock** — costs **1e35 years** of stored game time. Increases the softcap to Tickspeed Upgrades gained from Time Dimensions by **100,000**.
- **Second unlock** — costs **1e40 years**. Lets you finally unlock The Nameless Ones' Reality.

Completing The Nameless Ones' Reality unlocks **Tesseracts** — see the Tesseracts entry.

The Nameless Ones will not directly unlock the next Celestial.
