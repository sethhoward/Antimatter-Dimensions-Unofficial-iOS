**Lai'tela** is the sixth Celestial, unlocked by purchasing the appropriate Imaginary Upgrade for **5e16 iM**.

Lai'tela introduces a new currency — **Dark Matter** — which provides a multiplier to Continuum's effect based on the highest amount of Dark Matter you've ever had. Dark Matter is produced by **Dark Matter Dimensions**, cascading like every other dimension. Unlike the others, there are only **four** Dark Matter Dimensions. You start with the first one unlocked; the higher ones unlock via Imaginary Upgrades. When you unlock a dimension, you get **1** of it for free; you can't gain more without production from the next tier up.

Each Dark Matter Dimension, on a tick interval, generates two things: Dark Matter (or the next lower DMD) and another resource — **Dark Energy**. Dark Matter and DMD production per interval equals (Dark Matter multiplier × number of dimensions owned). Dark Energy production is independent of dimension count. Dark Energy is used to produce **Singularities** — see the Singularities entry.

## Interval upgrades and Ascension

Dark Matter Dimensions can have their interval upgraded down to a minimum of **10 ms**, at which point further interval upgrades are unavailable. You can choose to **ascend** Dark Matter Dimensions that reach the minimum. Initial ascension multiplies Dark Matter gain by **2e6** and Dark Energy by **5000**, and multiplies the interval by **1200**. The interval can then be upgraded again. Reaching **10 ms** again lets you ascend further.

## Annihilation

An Imaginary Upgrade unlocks a prestige called **Annihilation**. Annihilation resets your Dark Matter and Dark Matter Dimensions, but adds to a permanent multiplier to Dark Matter that applies to all Dark Matter Dimensions. You can Annihilate as many times as you want — the additions to the multiplier stack additively, and there is no need to Annihilate for a greater bonus each time. You need at least **1e60 Dark Matter** to Annihilate.

## Lai'tela's Reality

Lai'tela has a Reality that gives a multiplier to DMD Dark Matter power based on how well you perform. Completing the Reality in under **30 seconds** permanently disables your *highest* available Dark Matter Dimension during further attempts. Disabling all of your dimensions by completing the Reality in under 30 seconds **eight times** also gives a **×8** multiplier to Dark Energy gain.

Lai'tela will not directly unlock the next Celestial.
