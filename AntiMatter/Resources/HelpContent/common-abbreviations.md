The game throws a lot of abbreviations at you as you progress. Here's the full list of currencies, content types, and mechanics you'll encounter. Entries appear as their content unlocks — if you don't see an abbreviation here yet, you haven't reached the thing it names.

## Dimensions and resets

- **AD**: Antimatter Dimension
- **AM**: Antimatter
- [if:infinity] **ND**: Normal Dimension (synonym for AD, used in older notes)
- [if:infinity] **DB**: Dimension Boost
- [if:infinity] **AG**: Antimatter Galaxy

- [if:infinity] **NC**: Normal Challenge
- [if:infinity] **IC**: Infinity Challenge
- [if:infinity] **ID**: Infinity Dimension
- [if:replicanti] **RG**: Replicanti Galaxy
