**Ra** is the fifth Celestial, unlocked by completing all of V's Achievements. Ra uses their memories to revive positive effects from previous Celestials in stronger forms. Over time, you unlock the previous four Celestials *within* Ra. Each one offers additional upgrades themed around their original mechanics.

Each previous Celestial inside Ra gains **levels** by using **memories**. Memories are generated passively over time from **Memory Chunks**. Memory Chunks can only be gained while inside Ra's Reality, where Chunks are generated passively based on certain resource totals.

If you're storing real time, you will not gain Memory Chunks inside Ra's Reality — but Memories continue to be generated normally.

Reaching a total of **20** levels across all Celestials unlocks **Remembrance**, which lets you pick a particular Celestial to gain more Chunks for while inside Ra's Reality.

Memories can be spent on three things — an increase to Memory Chunk gain, an increase to Memory gain, and leveling up the Celestial itself. You start with only Teresa unlocked. Each subsequent Celestial inside Ra unlocks once you reach **level 8** with the previous Celestial. Levels are capped at **25**.

## What each Celestial does inside Ra

- **Teresa** unlocks the ability to *charge* your Infinity Upgrades, making them much stronger. Also improves Glyph effects once you reach certain thresholds in Glyph sacrifice value.
- **Effarig** (level **2**) unlocks **Glyph Alchemy** — see its own help entries — and later makes Effarig Glyphs stronger while gradually removing almost all random elements of Glyph generation.
- **The Nameless Ones** unlocks additional Black-Hole-charging mechanics and makes Black Holes significantly stronger.
- **V** unlocks **Triad Studies** — new studies near the bottom of the tree that cost Space Theorems. Each Triad Study requires you to also have the three nearby studies. Also unlocks a smaller set of more difficult V-Achievements that give extra Space Theorems.

Ra will not directly unlock the next Celestial.
