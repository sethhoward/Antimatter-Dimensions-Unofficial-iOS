Each Achievement has a requirement to unlock. Once unlocked, some Achievements give a reward — requirements and rewards vary widely in both difficulty and benefit.

In addition to any specific rewards, each Achievement awards a **×1.03** multiplier to all Antimatter Dimensions. Every fully completed row also gives another **×1.25**. The total multiplier from all Achievements is shown above the grid.

Some Achievements appear blurred — these are **Secret Achievements**. They offer no gameplay benefits, just fun. Tap a Secret Achievement to see a hint about how to attain it.
