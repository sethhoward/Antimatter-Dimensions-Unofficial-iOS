Eternity Challenges are unlocked by the Time Study Tree. Each requires a certain amount of Time Theorems plus a secondary requirement you must meet to unlock the challenge in the first place.

When you enter an Eternity Challenge, your goal becomes reaching a target IP. After completing the challenge, you do not need to have the EC's *Time Study* unlocked for the reward to apply. The rewards are similar to Time Studies but often stronger and permanent — you don't spend Time Theorems to keep the effect.

You can only have one Eternity Challenge unlocked at a time.

Each EC can be completed up to **five** times. After each completion, the reward grows stronger but the goal for the next completion also rises. The secondary unlock requirement increases each time too. The Time Theorem cost does not increase.

Completing an EC's secondary requirement *removes* it as a study requirement until you next complete that EC — so you only need to satisfy the secondary requirement *once*. You can therefore unlock an EC with one set of studies and then respec into a different set to actually beat it. **EC11 and EC12 are exceptions**: their Dimension path restrictions remain even after a respec.
