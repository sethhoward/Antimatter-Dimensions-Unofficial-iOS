**Teresa** is the first Celestial, unlocked by Achievement 147 — which requires obtaining all Reality Upgrades.

On Teresa's tab there's a bar with a **Pour RM** button above it. This places your Reality Machines into Teresa's container for a Reality Machine multiplier. **RM poured into the container cannot be retrieved.** When you reach **1e14 RM** inside the container, you unlock **Teresa's Reality**.

Completing Teresa's Reality multiplies your Glyph Sacrifice based on the antimatter you gained during the run.

Completing Teresa's Reality is only the first step — you need to keep pouring RM to progress further. Once you reach **1e24 RM** in the container, you unlock the next Celestial.

Teresa's Reality can be entered again after completing it. The reward gets stronger if you reach a higher antimatter total on a repeat run.
