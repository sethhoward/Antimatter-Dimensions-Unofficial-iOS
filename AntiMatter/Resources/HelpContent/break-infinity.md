Once you **Break Infinity**, the **1.8e308** antimatter cap is gone — you can start gaining more than **1 IP** per crunch depending on how far past it you push.

You now gain about **1.78 IP** for crunching at **1.8e308** antimatter. IP gained scales by **×10** for every additional factor of **1.8e308** you reach (continuously). The final value is rounded down to the nearest integer *after* all multipliers apply.

After breaking, the antimatter cost of all Dimensions starts increasing faster once they pass **1.8e308**. The cost *between* upgrades increases by **×10** *per upgrade* above **1.8e308**, and a similar scaling applies to Tickspeed Upgrade costs.
