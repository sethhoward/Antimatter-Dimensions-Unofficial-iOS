When you reach **100%** Recursion / Dispersion / Destruction, you unlock the **Galaxy Generator** — which passively generates Galaxies.

Generated Galaxies are like Replicanti Galaxies and Tachyon Galaxies: they affect tickspeed as if they were Antimatter Galaxies *but do not* increase the cost of your next Antimatter Galaxy.

You also unlock five new upgrades. The first increases the base amount of generated Galaxies. The other four give multipliers to that base amount. The first two upgrades can be purchased with antimatter and Generated Galaxies. **Replicanti and Tachyon Galaxies cannot be spent on these upgrades.**

The Galaxy Generator has a maximum number of Galaxies it can generate, which can only be raised by draining Rifts once the current cap has been reached.
