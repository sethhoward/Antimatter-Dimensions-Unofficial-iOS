**Glyph Alchemy** unlocks at Effarig level **2** within Ra. It lets you consume Glyphs by *refining* them into Alchemy Resources associated with their type. You refine Glyphs by setting your Sacrifice Type to something other than "Always Sacrifice" on the Glyphs tab, then sacrificing normally.

Each Alchemy Resource has a unique effect, visible on the Alchemy tab.

In addition to their other attributes, Glyphs now have a **refinement value** which determines how much of the associated Alchemy Resource they're worth. The value is based on the cube of the Glyph's level — scaled so that a level **10,000** Glyph corresponds to **10,000** Alchemy Resources. A single Glyph only gives **5%** of this value when refined. These figures are for **100%** rarity Glyphs; lower-rarity Glyphs share the same cap but contribute proportionally less. A **50%** rarity Glyph, for example, gives only half as much.

Alchemy Resources can't be gained indefinitely. There's a per-resource cap based on the *highest* refinement value of all Glyphs of that type you've refined. Example: if your highest-level refined Time Glyph was level **8000** (refinement value: that level's cube, scaled), your Time Resource cap stays at that value until you refine another Time Glyph with a higher refinement value — no matter how many lower-level Time Glyphs you refine.
